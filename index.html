Welcome

More than a home. We are family.

Faith Rwams AFH was founded by Jackline Nyoro and her family with a heart for caring for others. For the past four years, our family has been building experience in adult family home care. Faith Rwams AFH is our second home, and we are excited to continue growing while creating a place where residents and their families can feel safe, respected, and cared for.

To us, this is more than a business. It is a family. We believe that every person deserves to be treated with kindness, dignity, patience, and genuine care. When you choose Faith Rwams AFH, we want you and your family to feel confident that your loved one is welcomed into a home where we care for them as we would care for our own family.

We look forward to growing this journey together and earning the trust of every family we have the privilege of serving.
